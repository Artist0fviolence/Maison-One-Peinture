How much does it cost?

It depends on the size and condition of the space, so we quote after the free visit. Your written quote is itemized.

Do I need to move furniture?

No. We move it, cover it and put it back.

How long does a project take?

A typical 3½ takes about 2 days, prep included. Your quote gives exact start and finish dates.

Do you paint exteriors?

No — interiors only.

What does the warranty cover?

For 3 years, we fix any peeling, bubbling, cracking or adhesion problem caused by our prep or painting, at no cost. It doesn’t cover normal wear, impacts and scuffs, water or humidity damage, or touch-ups done by you or anyone else.

Where do you work?

Anywhere on the island of Montreal — from the West Island to the East End.
